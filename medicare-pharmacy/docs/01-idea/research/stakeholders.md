# Stakeholder map (non-monetized track)

| Stakeholder | Goal | Fear | Influence | Signs off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner / Administrator | True cash and profit; no theft; peace of mind on backups | Losing data; staff fraud; being fined at inspection | Sponsor, final say | Brief, design, go-live, restore drills |
| Pharmacist-in-charge (Manager) | Right batch dispensed; registers in order; fast approvals | Expired or Schedule B/D sale without record | High | Medicine master, approval thresholds, register format |
| Cashiers / Dispensers (most PCs) | Fast billing, no blame for stock gaps | Being slowed down at rush; mistakes blamed on them | Daily users, adoption risk | POS usability pilot |
| Stock operator | Quick goods-in from supplier bills | Typing 40 lines of batch and expiry per invoice | Medium | Purchase entry flow |
| Accountant (often part-time) | Ledgers that tie out; month-end without spreadsheets | Double-counted payments | Medium | Cashbook, ledgers, profit report |
| Local IT helper / vendor | Easy install, few support calls | Windows 7 PCs, network issues | Medium | Installer, firewall, backup docs |
| Drug inspector (external) | Records for 3 years; Schedule B/D register | - | Regulatory | Register and stock reports |
| Customers (credit) | Correct balance, receipt | Wrong dues | Low | - |
| Suppliers / distributors | Payment on time; returns by batch | Disputed balances | Low | Supplier statement |

## Adoption risks
- Cashiers return to the old way if a scan takes more than ~1 s or the screen needs the mouse.
- The stock operator rejects batch-per-line entry if every line must be typed. GS1 2D scanning and "repeat last batch" defaults reduce this.
- The owner stops trusting reports after one wrong number, so reconciliation checks must run nightly and show a visible status.
